# TikTok videos

Three vertical videos (1080×1920, 30 fps, 15–18 s), recorded from the real app with real prices.
They have **no music on purpose**: add a trending sound inside TikTok (tap **Add sound** when
posting, pick one from "Trending" or "For you"), at low volume under the captions. Sounds added in the
app are licensed for TikTok and get more reach than music baked into the file.

## 1. `1-pov-150-weekend.mp4` · POV: €150 for the weekend
Florence student, €150 budget → the app finds every trip that fits → Pisa & Lucca for €106, with the
day-by-day plan and where to book it.

**Caption:**
> POV: it's Thursday and you still haven't planned the weekend 😭 this finds every trip you can
> actually afford from wherever you're studying (free, link in bio) ✈️
> #studyabroad #florence #studyabroadlife #budgettravel #weekendtrip #italytravel #erasmus

**Sound idea:** a "POV" or "this changed my life" style trending sound.

## Posting tips
- **Post 2–3 times a week**, around 6–9 pm in your audience's time zone. Students abroad are mostly
  in Europe, so evenings in Central European Time.
- **Reply to comments with a video:** "From Barcelona?" → a new countdown for Barcelona. Ask Claude
  for one for any city; the prices come straight from the app.
- **Bio:** "Cheap weekend trips for students abroad ✈️ Free planner ↓" and `weekender-trips.com`
  in the link field.
- **First 3 seconds matter most:** every video opens on a big hook caption. Keep it that way if you
  re-cut them in CapCut.
