# Making money with Weekender

Every plan already has a **Book this trip** list (flights, train/bus tickets, hostel, tours, eSIM).
Travelpayouts **Drive** turns those links into affiliate links, but only for programs you've joined.
Plus two bigger plays that don't depend on traffic.

## This week: make every booking button earn (about 30 min)
In Travelpayouts → **Programs**, join each of these (some need approval, which takes a few days):

| Program | Button on the site | Typical commission |
|---|---|---|
| **Kiwi.com** | Flights (main button) | ~3% of the booking |
| **Aviasales** | Flights (compare) | already joined |
| **Omio** | Train, bus and ferry tickets | ~6% of the booking |
| **Hostelworld** | Hostel | % of the deposit |
| **GetYourGuide** | Tours & tickets | ~8% |
| **Airalo** | Mobile data (eSIM) | ~10% |

Then open a plan on the live site, tap each button, and check that the address briefly passes
through a Travelpayouts/`tp.media` link: that means Drive is tracking it. In a few days the
Travelpayouts dashboard shows clicks per brand; that tells you which buttons people use.

Two extras worth doing once you're approved:
- **GetYourGuide partner ID:** send it to Claude to add to the tours links (`GYG_PARTNER_ID`).
- **Omio route links:** Omio's deep links need city IDs from its "Deep link Creator" file in the
  Travelpayouts Omio program. Download it and send it to Claude, and the train/bus button can
  open the exact route instead of Omio's home page (more people book when the search is filled in).

## Next: student tour companies (the best fit)
Companies like Bus2Alps, Smartrip, Florence for Fun and Erasmus travel agencies sell weekend
trips to exactly your users. Two ways they pay:
1. **Affiliate:** many have referral programs (ask: "do you have an affiliate or ambassador
   program?"). You earn per booking from a "Book a guided trip" link on matching cities.
2. **Featured trip:** a clearly labeled "Featured" trip in the list for their city, for a monthly
   fee. Start low (€50–100/month per city) and offer the first month free.

**Email to send (copy, then fill in the brackets):**

> Subject: Free student trip planner, partnership idea
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name], a student at [school]. I built Weekender (weekender-trips.com), a free planner
> that shows study-abroad students the best cheap weekend trips from their city, with real prices
> and day-by-day plans.
>
> Your trips are exactly what our users look for. Would you be open to (a) an affiliate link from
> plans for the cities you run trips to, or (b) a labeled "Featured trip" from [city]? I'd be happy
> to offer the first month free so you can see the clicks.
>
> Here's what a plan looks like: weekender-trips.com/from/[city]/
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

## Then: study-abroad offices and program providers
Offices want resources for students' free weekends. Offer Weekender free first (traffic + a
testimonial), then a paid **"Weekender for [School]"** version: their logo, their city, their
safety links and travel-registration reminder, for a flat fee per semester.

> Subject: A free weekend-trip planner for your students in [city]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm a [school] student and I built Weekender (weekender-trips.com), a free planner that shows
> students the best cheap weekend trips from [city], with real prices, travel times and day-by-day
> plans. Would it be useful to share with your students before the semester? I'd love any feedback
> from your team, and I'm happy to make a version with your program's branding and safety
> information.
>
> Best,
> [your name]

## Later
- **Premium** (a few euros a semester): group trips, unlimited price alerts, offline plans.
  Worth it once you have regular users.
- **Skip display ads:** at student-site traffic they pay very little and clutter the design.

## Track it
Keep one simple sheet: date, visitors (Cloudflare Web Analytics), Travelpayouts clicks and
bookings, emails sent and replies. Check it every Monday when the new fares come in.
